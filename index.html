Our mission

Collaborative community

A home for every aerospace interest, from pilots and space nerds to aviation history buffs. Socials, movie nights, networking, and a group of people who get excited about the same things you do.

Professional outreach

Speakers from NASA, industry, and academia: commercial, defense, and space professionals, pilots, UW professors, and grad students. Plus facility tours and local companies.

Skill development

Study sessions, resume reviews, peer mentoring, and career events with Engineering Career Services to help you land that first internship.

Looking for a hands-on build team? Check out WISP and SAE Aero Design. Members of those teams are welcome here too.

Get involved

Join the chapter

Membership is free and open to every UW–Madison student. Here's how to get on board.

  1. Join our Slack. Meeting times, speaker announcements, and the rest of the chapter are all there.
  2. Fill out the interest form to get on our email list.
  3. Come to a meeting. Bring a friend. No experience required.
  4. Optional: become a national AIAA student member for journals, conferences, scholarships, and the national career network.
